# Privacy policy – Export for Claude

Last updated: 2026-11-26

Export for Claude is a browser extension that saves the claude.ai conversation
you have open as Markdown, JSON or PDF, and downloads the files in that
conversation as a zip.

## What the extension accesses

When you click one of its buttons, the extension reads the open conversation
and its files from claude.ai. It does this from your browser, as you, using the
session you are already signed in with. It only runs on `https://claude.ai/*`.

## What it collects

Nothing. The extension:

- does not send your conversations, files or any other data to the developer
  or to any third party,
- has no server, no account, no analytics, no tracking and no ads,
- does not store your conversations; the export is handed straight to your
  browser's download dialog, and you choose where it is saved.

The only network requests the extension makes go to claude.ai, to read the
conversation and files you asked to export.

## Permissions

- **Access to claude.ai**: to read the conversation and its files when you ask
  for an export.
- **Downloads**: to save the exported file through your browser's Save As
  dialog.
- **Offscreen document**: to turn the export into a file inside the extension
  so the browser can download it. Nothing leaves your computer.
